I am an Earth observation researcher focused on machine learning methods for remote sensing data. My interests center on applying machine learning to leverage geospatial data for real-world applications and addressing related methodological issues. My current work involves segmenting building footprints and other objects from drone and satellite imagery to help assess mosquito-borne disease risks in African cities.

Recently, I submitted my PhD thesis under the supervision of Christian Igel, with co-supervision from Stefan Oehmcke at the University of Copenhagen, Denmark. I am now a postdoctoral researcher at the same research group, where I work closely with Christian Igel, Nico Lang, Ankit Kariryaa and Rasmus Fensholt.

Previously, I completed my Master's at the University of Twente, Netherlands (joint education program with Indian Institute of Remote Sensing, ISRO, India), and my Bachelor's at Andhra University College of Engineering, India. Before starting my PhD, I worked at several organizations, including Mapbox, Wildlife Institute of India and CropIn. Please check my Curriculum Vitae for more details.
